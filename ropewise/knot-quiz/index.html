Knot quiz: can you name these five knots?

This free knot quiz shows you five finished knots from the Ropewise guide and asks you to name each one from four choices. Where possible, the wrong names come from the same family of knots, so it’s a fair test. Every answer comes with a link to that knot’s step-by-step diagrams.

Name that knot

Look at each knot, pick a name, then open the answer to check it.

  1. Question 1 of 5 Which knot is this?

    1. Slip knot
    2. Reef knot
    3. Constrictor knot
    4. Double overhand knot
    Show the answer

    It’s the reef knot. A flat binding knot that ties the two ends of a cord snugly around a bundle or parcel.

  2. Question 2 of 5 Which knot is this?

    1. Elastic bracelet knot
    2. Ian knot
    3. Double overhand knot
    4. Sliding bracelet knot
    Show the answer

    It’s the Ian knot. A very fast way to tie the standard shoelace bow: make two loops at once, then pull each lace through the other loop.

  3. Question 3 of 5 Which knot is this?

    1. Rolling hitch
    2. Anchor bend
    3. Figure-eight stopper knot
    4. Sheet bend
    Show the answer

    It’s the sheet bend. Joins two ropes, even of different thickness or stiffness: the thinner rope wraps round a bight in the thicker one.

  4. Question 4 of 5 Which knot is this?

    1. Timber hitch
    2. Taut-line hitch
    3. Siberian hitch
    4. Trucker's hitch
    Show the answer

    It’s the Siberian hitch. A quick-release hitch for tying a line to a tree or post, released in one pull.

  5. Question 5 of 5 Which knot is this?

    1. Surgeon's knot
    2. Improved clinch knot
    3. Uni knot
    4. Blood knot
    Show the answer

    It’s the uni knot. Turns taken round a doubled line inside a loop: one easy method that ties line to hooks, lures and swivels.
