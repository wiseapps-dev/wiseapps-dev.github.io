Which knot for the job: guides

Short guides to choosing a knot. Each one starts from a job, such as joining two ropes, tying a line to a post or fishing line to a hook, and compares the knots that do it: what each holds, how it unties and when to pick it. Every knot links to its step-by-step diagrams.

  • How to tie two ropes togetherThe knots to tie two ropes together: the sheet bend for unequal ropes, the double sheet bend for slippery rope, and why not the reef knot.
  • Best knot for tying a rope to a post, rail or ringWhich knot ties a rope to a post, rail, ring or tree: round turn and two half hitches, clove hitch, bowline, cleat hitch, anchor bend and more.
  • Loop knots: how to tie a fixed loop in a ropeWhich loop knot to tie: the bowline for a fixed loop at the end of a rope, plus the dropper loop and the non-slip loop for fishing line.
  • Tensioning knots: tarps, guy lines and bundlesKnots to tension a tarp, guy line or clothesline: the trucker's hitch to pull a line tight, plus the taut-line hitch, round turn and constrictor.
  • Fishing knots: which knot for hooks, leaders and reelsWhich fishing knot to use: Palomar, improved clinch and uni for hooks; FG, double uni and Albright for leaders; the arbor knot for the reel.
