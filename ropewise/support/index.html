Support

Email wiseapps.help+ropewise@gmail.com. A real person replies, usually within two business days. Spotted a mistake in a diagram, or want a knot added? Tell us which one.

Before you start

Ropewise is a learning guide for everyday knots. Don't rely on it for climbing, rescue, lifting or holding people, overhead loads, or anything where a failure could hurt someone. Its climbing chapter shows how those knots are tied; it doesn't teach climbing.

Knots can slip, jam or weaken a rope. Practise first, and check your knot every time.

When it matters, use rated equipment, follow the maker's instructions and get trained by a qualified person.

Ropewise can contain mistakes and can't check your knot. You use it at your own risk, to the extent the law allows. Read the terms of use.

Ropewise Pro

The app is free with 12 essential knots. Ropewise Pro is one optional purchase, with no subscription, that unlocks every knot, every quiz game, Refresh rounds and the practice reminder. New phone or reinstalled? Tap any knot marked Pro, or go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases.

Refunds

Apple handles App Store refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play refunds go through your Google Play order history.
