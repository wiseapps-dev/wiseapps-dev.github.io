How to tie the right knot, step by step

Ropewise is a pocket guide to 40 knots for boating, fishing, camping, home and garden, and craft. Choose what you need to do and it points you to the knots for the job. Every knot is drawn one move at a time, with plain words for where the end goes: over, under, behind or through.

What do you need to do?

Make a fixed loop3 knots

At the end of a rope, a bowline unties easily after loading; leave a long tail, because it can shake loose when slack. In fishing line, tie a dropper loop or a non-slip loop.

  • Bowlinefixed loop at the end
  • Dropper loopside loop for a second hook
  • Non-slip loop knotloop that lets a lure swing

Loop knots compared

Join two ropes3 knots

A sheet bend joins ropes of different sizes and unties easily; leave long tails. A reef knot is not for this: it can capsize and slip apart.

  • Sheet bendjoins two unequal ropes
  • Double uni knotjoins two fishing lines
  • FG knotslim braid-to-leader join

How to tie two ropes together

Tie to a post or ring7 knots

A round turn and two half hitches holds a steady pull and unties even under load. A clove hitch is quicker but can slip when the pull changes direction.

  • Clove hitchquick hitch to a rail or post
  • Bowlinefixed loop at the end
  • Siberian hitchquick-release to a tree
  • Cleat hitchline to a horn cleat
  • Anchor bendrope to an anchor ring
  • Timber hitchdrags a log along the ground

Best knot for tying to a post

Tension a line or tarp4 knots

A trucker's hitch pulls a line far tighter than you can by hand, then locks off. A taut-line hitch lets you re-tension a guy line without untying it. Vehicle and trailer loads need tie-down straps, not knots.

  • Trucker's hitchtensions a line or tarp
  • Taut-line hitchadjustable guy line
  • Round turn and two half hitchesrope to a ring, rail or post
  • Constrictor knotbinds a sack or bundle tight

Fishing knots: which one when

Climbing7 knots

Climbing knots. These knots are used in climbing and other activities where a mistake can kill. Ropewise shows how each knot is tied. It doesn't teach climbing and doesn't replace instruction. Learn them from a qualified instructor, and have your knots checked, before you rely on one. Follow your equipment maker's instructions.

Not a substitute for instruction. Not for life-safety use without training.
