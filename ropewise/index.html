How Ropewise works

  1. Tell it the jobJoin two lines, tie up a boat, tension a guy line or make a fixed loop. Ropewise lists the knots that do it, easiest first.
  2. Follow one move at a timeEach step has its own drawing and a line that says where the end goes. Blue is the standing part, gold the working end, and a dashed arrow shows the next move.
  3. Practise until it sticks“Name that knot” is free. Pro adds two more quiz games and Refresh rounds that bring back the knots you’re due to practise.

Make a fixed loop3 knots

At the end of a rope, a bowline unties easily after loading; leave a long tail, because it can shake loose when slack. In fishing line, tie a dropper loop or a non-slip loop.

Join two ropes3 knots

A sheet bend joins ropes of different sizes and unties easily; leave long tails. A reef knot is not for this: it can capsize and slip apart.

  • Sheet bendjoins two unequal ropes

Tension a line or tarp4 knots

A trucker's hitch pulls a line far tighter than you can by hand, then locks off. A taut-line hitch lets you re-tension a guy line without untying it. Vehicle and trailer loads need tie-down straps, not knots.

Questions

Is Ropewise free?

Yes. The app is free to download with 12 essential knots, the “Which knot?” finder, left-handed mode, favourites and the “Name that knot” game. Ropewise Pro is one optional purchase of US$4.99 that unlocks all 40 knots, every quiz game, Refresh rounds and the practice reminder. There’s no subscription.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Every knot and every drawing is in the app, so it works completely offline, on the boat or in the bush, and it’s a small download.

I’m left-handed. Will the diagrams make sense?

Turn on left-handed mode and every diagram is mirrored, so you can tie the knot the way your hands want to.

How are the diagrams made?

Every diagram is drawn in code from our own knot data, one move at a time. The standing part and the working end are in different colours, and a dashed arrow shows where the end goes next.

Does Ropewise collect my data?

No. There’s no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your saved knots and settings stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Is it a substitute for instruction?

No. Ropewise is a learning guide for everyday knots. Practise first and check the knot every time. Not for climbing or for holding a person. Read the terms of use.

When can I get it?

Ropewise is coming soon for iPhone and Android. Until then, 12 essential knots are on this website step by step, and every other knot with its finished drawing and first steps.
