How to tie a San Diego jam knot

San Diego jam knot. Turns taken back over a doubled line, then the tag tucked through the loops at both ends. A compact hook knot. Use it for tying hooks, lures and swivels to mono or fluorocarbon, or heavier fluorocarbon leaders, where a clinch is hard to seat.

At a glance

Use
Hook knot for heavier fluoro.
  • Tying hooks, lures and swivels to mono or fluorocarbon
  • Heavier fluorocarbon leaders, where a clinch is hard to seat
Good
Compact, and seats well in heavier mono and fluorocarbon.
Watch out
More steps than a clinch, and the turns must be bedded neatly before tightening.
Unties
No: cut it off and retie
Tie it in
Mono or fluoro
Difficulty
Medium, 5 steps

Every knot weakens the line it is tied in. Wet it, snug it down slowly and test it with a hard pull before you fish it.

Common mistakes

  • Tightening before the turns are bedded. Lay them neatly first.
  • Pulling it up dry. Wet the knot before you snug it down.

Origin and references

Origin
Also called the reverse clinch or Heiliger knot; it was popularised by long-range tuna fishermen in San Diego.
