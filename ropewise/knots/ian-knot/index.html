How to tie an Ian knot

Ian knot. A very fast way to tie the standard shoelace bow: make two loops at once, then pull each lace through the other loop. Use it for tying shoes, boots and runners quickly. Ties the same balanced bow as the usual method, much faster; unties with a pull on either end.

Every knot weakens the rope it is tied in, and any knot can slip or jam. Check it each time you tie it.

Common mistakes

  • Twisting a loop the wrong way. The red end crosses in front, the blue end behind (step 3), or the bow will not form.
  • Skipping the starting knot. Tie the first crossing and pull it snug before you make the loops.

Origin and references

Origin
Invented by Ian Fieggen, 'Professor Shoelace', in 1982; his first sketches are dated 30 June 1982.
