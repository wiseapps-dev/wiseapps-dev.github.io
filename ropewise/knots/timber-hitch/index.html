How to tie a timber hitch

Timber hitch. A quick hitch for dragging a log or bundle along the ground: a loop that tightens as you pull and grips with a few twists of the end. Use it for dragging a log or a bundle of poles along the ground, or starting a diagonal lashing.

  • Easy
  • 4 steps
  • Camping & outdoor knots

Take care. Only for dragging along the ground. It can loosen as soon as the pull eases, so never use it to lift, lower or hang anything.

Step by step

Practise first and check the knot every time. Not for climbing or for holding a person.

Very quick to tie, holds under a steady pull and falls apart easily once the load is off.
Watch out

Rope or cord
Difficulty
Easy, 4 steps

Every knot weakens the rope it is tied in, and any knot can slip or jam. Check it each time you tie it.

Common mistakes

  • Too few twists. Twist the end round its own part about three times.
  • Relying on it alone for a long drag. Add a half hitch further along the log.

Origin and references

Origin
