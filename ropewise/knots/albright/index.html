How to tie an Albright knot

Albright knot. Joins a light line to a much heavier one by wrapping the light line round a loop doubled in the heavy line. Use it for joining braid or light mono to a heavy mono or fluoro leader, or joining backing to a fly line.

  • Fiddly
  • 5 steps

Step by step

The two colours are the two ropes or lines. A dashed red arrow shows where the end goes next.

Practise first and check the knot every time. Not for climbing or for holding a person.

At a glance

Use
Light line to a heavy leader.
  • Joining braid or light mono to a heavy mono or fluoro leader
  • Joining backing to a fly line
Good
Copes with lines of very different thickness and stays slim enough for rod guides.
Watch out
Takes practice to wrap neatly, and must be snugged down carefully or it can slip, especially in braid.
Unties
No: cut it off and retie
Tie it in
Mono, braid or fluoro
Difficulty
Fiddly, 5 steps
Also called
Albright special

Every knot weakens the line it is tied in. Wet it, snug it down slowly and test it with a hard pull before you fish it.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the wraps cross or pile up. Lay each turn beside the last, working towards the loop end.
  • Pulling only the main lines. Slide the wraps snug to the loop end with the light line and its tag first (step 5), then pull both lines tight.

Origin and references

Origin
An angling knot for joining lines of very different diameters. Neither source checked says who devised it or when.
