How to tie an elastic bracelet knot

Elastic bracelet knot. The standard knot for closing a stretchy bead bracelet: a reef knot with an extra turn in the first throw so it holds while you finish it. Use it for stretch bead bracelets on clear or coloured elastic cord, or bead bracelets on slippery cord, where a reef knot would loosen.

At a glance

Use
Closes a stretch bracelet.
  • Stretch bead bracelets on clear or coloured elastic cord
  • Bead bracelets on slippery cord, where a reef knot would loosen
Good
The extra turn grips, so the first throw stays tight while you tie the second. Small enough to hide inside a bead.
Watch out
Elastic is slippery: pre-stretch the cord, tighten firmly and add a dab of jewellery glue.
Unties
Not meant to: it is glued and trimmed
Tie it in
Elastic cord or cord
Difficulty
Medium, 6 steps
Also called
Stretch bracelet knot

Every knot weakens the rope it is tied in, and any knot can slip or jam. Check it each time you tie it.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the pre-stretch. Stretch the elastic before tying, or the bracelet sags and the knot loosens.
  • Tying the second throw the same way as the first. It crosses back the other way, right over left.

Origin and references

Origin
This is the surgeon's knot (Ashley #463): a reef knot whose first throw has an extra turn. It is named after its use by surgeons and is the usual finishing knot recommended for stretch-cord jewellery.
