How to tie a sliding bracelet knot

Sliding bracelet knot. An adjustable closure for cord bracelets: one end is coiled round the other cord so the knot slides to open and close. Use it for friendship and cord bracelets without a clasp, or adjustable necklaces and anklets on waxed cotton or nylon cord.

At a glance

Use
Adjustable bracelet closure.
  • Friendship and cord bracelets without a clasp
  • Adjustable necklaces and anklets on waxed cotton or nylon cord
  • Tie one with each end for a closure that adjusts from both sides
Good
No clasp needed; slips over the hand, then slides snug. Easy to adjust or undo.
Watch out
Very slippery cord can creep open; leave tails long enough to grip.
Unties
Yes, easily

Also called
Adjustable bracelet knot, Sliding knot

Every knot weakens the rope it is tied in, and any knot can slip or jam. Check it each time you tie it.

Common mistakes

  • Wrapping away from the bend. Wrap towards it, inside the loop.
  • Trimming the tails too short to grip, especially in slippery cord.

Origin and references

Origin
A jewellery-making closure with no documented inventor in the sources checked. One end is wrapped round the other cord and tucked through its own loop, a uni-type coil that can slide.
