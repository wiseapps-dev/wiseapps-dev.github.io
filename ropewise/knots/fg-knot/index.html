How to tie an FG knot

FG knot. A very slim braid-to-leader join: the braid is woven round the leader in alternating wraps, then locked with half hitches. Use it for joining braid main line to a mono or fluoro leader, or casting a long leader out through the rod guides.

Every knot weakens the line it is tied in. Wet it, snug it down slowly and test it with a hard pull before you fish it.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the braid go slack while wrapping. Keep it tight or the wraps will not bite into the leader.
  • Trimming before you bed the wraps. Pull hard after the first half hitches (step 3), then finish and trim.

Origin and references

Origin
A modern braid-to-leader knot, also called the Sebile or GT knot. Animated Knots says Frenchman Patrick Sébile supposedly introduced it, and the meaning of 'FG' is uncertain.
