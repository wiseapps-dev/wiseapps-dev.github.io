How to tie an arbor knot

Arbor knot. Ties new line to an empty reel spool: an overhand round the standing line, locked by a stopper knot in the tag. Use it for spooling a reel with fresh line, or tying backing to a fly reel.

At a glance

Use
Line to an empty spool.
  • Spooling a reel with fresh line
  • Tying backing to a fly reel
Good
Quick, simple and slides down to grip the spool, with nothing bulky to snag the line on top.
Watch out
Can slip on a smooth spool with braid: put a layer of mono or tape under it first.
Unties
No: cut it off and retie
Tie it in
Mono, fluoro or braid
Difficulty
Easy, 4 steps

Every knot weakens the line it is tied in. Wet it, snug it down slowly and test it with a hard pull before you fish it.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the stopper knot in the tag (step 3), so the overhand can pull free.
  • Tying braid straight onto a smooth, bare spool, where it can slip. Put a layer of mono or tape underneath first.

Origin and references

Origin
A traditional angler's knot for tying line to a reel arbor, also used in bushcraft as the 'Canadian jam knot'. No inventor is documented in the sources checked.
