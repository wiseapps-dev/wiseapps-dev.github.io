How to tie a snell knot

Snell knot. Turns of line bound round the hook shank, so the pull comes straight down the shank. A favourite for bait and circle hooks, because the straight pull helps set the point. Use it for bait rigs, circle hooks and two-hook (tandem) rigs.

At a glance

Use
Line to a bait or circle hook.
  • Bait rigs and circle hooks, for a straight pull that sets the point
  • Two-hook (tandem) bait rigs
Good
Keeps the line in line with the shank, and helps circle hooks turn into the corner of the mouth.
Watch out
For hooks only, not lures or swivels, and slower to tie than a clinch or uni.
Unties
No: cut it off and retie
Tie it in
Mono, fluoro or braid
Difficulty
Medium, 4 steps

Every knot weakens the line it is tied in. Wet it, snug it down slowly and test it with a hard pull before you fish it.

Common mistakes

  • Using it on a lure or swivel. A snell is for hooks only.
  • Pulling only the tag. Slide the turns up to the eye, then pull the standing line hard to seat them.

Origin and references

Origin
One of the older fishing knots, originally invented for eyeless hooks.
