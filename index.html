Wise Apps

Seven small apps, each built to do one job well. They work offline, with no account, no ads and no tracking. Every app comes with free tools and guides you can use in your browser right now, from a baluster spacing calculator to tonight's planets. The apps come to iPhone first, then Android.

  • Gapwise

    Even baluster, picket and stair spacing, worked out.

    Free: Baluster spacing calculator · UK spindle spacing calculator

  • Benchwise

    Fair sub rotations and equal game time for kids' sport.

    Free: Equal playing time calculator · Sub rotation charts by sport

  • Scenewise

    The run-lines app for actors: your phone reads the cues.

    Free: Run lines online · First-letter memorisation tool

  • Ropewise

    Step-by-step knots for boating, fishing, camping and home.

    Free: How to tie a bowline · List of knots and their uses

  • Closetwise

    See everything you own: wardrobe, outfits and packing.

    Free: Cost per wear calculator · Packing list generator

  • Pencilwise

    Kids trace letters and numbers, stroke by stroke, in school handwriting styles.

    Free: Name tracing worksheet maker · Letter formation for every letter

  • Stellarwise

    Find stars, planets and the Moon tonight. Works offline.

    Free: Planets visible tonight · Moon phase on any date
