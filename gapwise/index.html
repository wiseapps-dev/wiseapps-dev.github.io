Questions

Is it a subscription?

No. Gapwise is free to download, and one optional in-app purchase, Gapwise Pro (US$7.99, once), unlocks everything. There are no ads and no account.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Free: the gap, count and centres for any run with the first 4 marks, stairs with the pass or fail check, and decking with the last board, in millimetres or inches. Pro, a one-time unlock: every mark, the cut list, the lengths to buy for a deck, saved jobs, and copy and share.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All the maths runs on your phone, so it works in a basement, on a roof or out bush.

Does it make my balustrade compliant?

No app can do that. The presets keep every gap under the sphere size you choose, but building rules vary by place and change over time. Check yours with your certifier, building surveyor or inspector.

Is there an Android version?

The iPhone version comes first. Android follows a few weeks later.

Can I use inches?

Yes. Tap "in" and every field takes feet, inches and fractions, like 4' 6 1/2".
