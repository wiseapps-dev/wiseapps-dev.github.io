Balustrade spacing calculator

Under the NCC, openings in an Australian balustrade must not let a 125 mm sphere through, so every gap has to be under 125 mm, not on it. For the count, take the length between posts minus 125, divide by the baluster width plus 125, and round up. If it comes out whole, add one.

The Australian rule in one line

Under the NCC, openings in a balustrade must not let a 125 mm sphere through. So every gap has to be under 125 mm, not equal to it. The calculator's AU preset keeps every gap under the limit.

When you need a balustrade

  • Where someone could fall 1 m or more from a deck, balcony, stair, landing or ramp.
  • Height: at least 1 m on decks, balconies and landings, and 865 mm above the stair nosings.
  • Where the fall is more than 4 m, horizontal rails between 150 mm and 760 mm above the floor mustn't make it easy to climb.

This is a summary, not legal advice. The balustrade gap rules guide has the clause numbers, the NZ and UK rules (100 mm) and more worked examples. Rules change and states adopt editions on different dates, so check with your building surveyor or certifier. Gapwise doesn't certify compliance.

Worked example

count = (2400 − 125) ÷ (42 + 125) = 13.6, round up to 14
gap   = (2400 − 14 × 42) ÷ 15     = 120.8 mm

2400 mm between posts with 42 mm balusters needs 14 balusters at 120.8 mm. Mark each one from the post face, not by stepping the gap along. If the count comes out as a whole number, the gap lands exactly on 125 mm, so add one.

A note on words

The balustrade is the whole barrier. Balusters (also called spindles or pickets) are the uprights. The handrail or banister is the rail on top, though plenty of people call the whole stair railing the banister.

Questions

What is the maximum balustrade gap in Australia?

A 125 mm sphere must not pass through any opening. That includes decorative balustrades, and on stairs the opening is measured above the nosing line.

Is the balustrade gap in New Zealand the same?

No. Under F4/AS1, barriers in housing must stop a 100 mm sphere. Use the NZ/UK preset on the baluster spacing calculator.

Does the calculator make my balustrade compliant?

No app can do that. The preset keeps every gap under 125 mm, but rules vary by building type and change over time. Check yours with your certifier, building surveyor or inspector.
