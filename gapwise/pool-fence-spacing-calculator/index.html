The gap rules for pool fences

The Queensland Building and Construction Commission says vertical elements such as posts, slats or wires can be up to 100 mm apart where the outside rails are at least 900 mm apart. The NSW Government's self-assessment checklist for pools built after 1 May 2013 (AS 1926.1-2012) says the gap between all vertical or near-vertical rails must be less than 100 mm. The calculator keeps every gap under 100 mm, which meets both.

The other checks on the same pages

  • Height: at least 1200 mm, measured from the finished ground level outside the pool (QBCC, NSW).
  • Gap underneath: no more than 100 mm from the finished ground level to the bottom of the fence (QBCC, NSW).
  • Climbing: QBCC asks for at least one gap of 900 mm or more between rails; the NSW checklist says there must be no hand or foot holds within 900 mm of the top of the fence.
  • Mesh or perforated panels: holes of 13 mm or less (NSW checklist).

Pool barrier rules save lives and differ by state, by the date the pool was built and by the type of fence. This page only spaces the bars; it isn't building advice and it can't tell you that a fence passes. Check your state's pool safety rules and have the barrier inspected by your council or a licensed pool safety inspector.

Worked example: one 2400 mm panel

The posts are 2400 mm apart and the bars are 25 mm wide. With bars between two posts there is a gap at both ends, so n bars make n + 1 gaps.

count   = (2400 − 100) ÷ (25 + 100) = 18.4, round up to 19
gap     = (2400 − 19 × 25) ÷ 20     = 96.3 mm
centres = 25 + 96.3                 = 121.3 mm

With 16 mm bars the same panel needs 20 at 99.1 mm. If the count comes out as a whole number, the gap lands exactly on 100 mm, so add one.

Questions

What is the maximum gap between pool fence bars?

Under 100 mm. NSW's checklist says less than 100 mm and QBCC says up to 100 mm apart; keeping every gap under 100 mm meets both. Other states have their own rules.

How high does a pool fence need to be?

At least 1200 mm from the finished ground level outside the pool, with no more than 100 mm under the bottom, according to QBCC and the NSW checklist.

Can I use this for a glass pool fence?

Only for the gaps between bars or posts. Glass panels, hinges and the gaps between panels have their own rules, so follow the maker's instructions and your inspector.
