Privacy

Gapwise does not collect any personal data. The app has no account, login, analytics, advertising or tracking. The lengths, jobs and settings you enter stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

  • The app has no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The lengths, jobs and settings you enter are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • If you share or copy a result, it goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Gapwise is free. The optional Pro unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play: Apple or Google handle the payment, and we never see your card or account. The app keeps a note on your device that Pro is unlocked, so it works offline, and never sends it anywhere.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The free calculator runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

If you email us for support, we use your email only to reply to you. We don't add it to a mailing list.

Who we are: Gapwise publishes the Gapwise app and this website. Questions: wiseapps.help+gapwise@gmail.com
