Stair calculator

To work out a stair, divide the floor-to-floor rise by the largest riser your rules allow and round up: that's the number of risers. Divide the rise by it for the riser height. There's one tread fewer than risers, so the total run is the going times one less than the risers.

Worked example: 2700 mm floor to floor

The floors are 2700 mm apart in an Australian house. The riser can be no more than 190 mm, the going must be 240 mm to 355 mm, and 2R + G must land between 550 mm and 700 mm.

risers   2700 ÷ 190 = 14.2, so 15
riser    2700 ÷ 15 = 180 mm
going    625 − 2 × 180 = 265 mm
2R + G   2 × 180 + 265 = 625 mm
run      14 × 265 = 3710 mm
angle    atan(180 ÷ 265) = 34.2°
stringer √(2700² + 3710²) = 4588 mm

15 risers need only 14 treads, because the top floor is the last step. If you only have 3.2 m of run, the going drops to 228 mm, under the 240 mm minimum. The calculator flags it and tells you the shortest run that works: 3360 mm.

The same 2700 mm rise under the other presets: 13 risers of 207.7 mm with a 231 mm going for an English private stair (kept under 42°), 15 risers with a 280 mm going in New Zealand, and 14 risers of 192.9 mm in Spain. In the US, 108 inches takes 14 risers of 7 23/32 inches on 10 inch treads.

Going, tread and 2R + G

The going is the level distance from one nosing to the next. A tread is usually a little deeper, because it overhangs the riser below. The IRC's tread depth is also measured nosing to nosing, so it's the same number.

2R + G is twice the riser plus the going. It keeps each step in proportion with a normal stride. The Australian and English rules allow 550 mm to 700 mm. When your run is free, the calculator picks the going that puts 2R + G at 625 mm, then keeps it inside the preset's limits.

Questions

How many steps do I need for a 2.7 m floor-to-floor height?

In an Australian house, 15 risers of 180 mm and 14 treads. Under the English private-stair rules it's 13 risers of 207.7 mm. Type your own rise into the calculator for the exact figures.

What is the maximum riser height?

190 mm in Australian houses, 7 3/4 inches under the IRC, 220 mm for a private stair in England, 190 mm for a main private stair in New Zealand, and 200 mm inside a home in Spain.

Is the stringer length the board I should buy?

It's the diagonal from the foot of the first riser to the top nosing. Buy a longer board, because the top and bottom cuts and the fixing at the top take extra.

Does the calculator make my stair compliant?

No app can do that. The presets check the riser, going and the other limits listed above, but handrails, headroom, landings and balustrades have rules too, and they vary by place. Check with your certifier, building surveyor or inspector.
