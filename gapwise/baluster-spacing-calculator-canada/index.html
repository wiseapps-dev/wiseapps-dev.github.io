The rule in one line

The British Columbia Building Code (2018, Article 9.8.8.5) says openings through guards must be of a size that prevents the passage of a spherical object 100 mm in diameter. The City of Kitchener's guide gives the same 100 mm limit under the Ontario Building Code. So every gap between balusters has to be under 100 mm, which is a little less than 4 inches (3 15/16 inches). The calculator's preset keeps every gap under it.

More from the British Columbia Building Code (2018)

  • When you need a guard: where the walking surface is more than 600 mm above the adjacent surface, or the surface within 1.2 m slopes more than 1 in 2 (9.8.8.1).
  • Height: 900 mm for guards inside a dwelling unit, and for exterior guards serving one dwelling unit where the walking surface is not more than 1800 mm above the finished ground. Other guards: 1070 mm (9.8.8.3).
  • Climbing: where a required guard protects a level more than 4.2 m above the next level, nothing between 140 mm and 900 mm above the floor may make it easy to climb (9.8.8.6).

This is a summary to help you plan, not building advice, and the calculator doesn't check that your work meets the rules. Rules vary by place and building type, change over time and are adopted on different dates. Check your local building code and your inspector, certifier or building control body before you build. Each province and territory adopts its own code and edition, and some cities add their own rules, so check the code that applies where you build.

Worked example: a 1.8 m deck section

The posts are 1800 mm apart and the balusters are 38 mm wide. With balusters between two posts there is a gap at both ends, so n balusters make n + 1 gaps.

count   = (1800 − 100) ÷ (38 + 100) = 12.3, round up to 13
gap     = (1800 − 13 × 38) ÷ 14     = 93.3 mm
centres = 38 + 93.3                 = 131.3 mm

In inches, an 8 foot section (96 inches between posts) with 1 1/2 inch balusters needs 17 at 3 29/32 inches. Tap "inches" and the calculator works in feet, inches and fractions. For balusters on the stair, use the balusters per tread calculator.

Questions

What is the maximum baluster spacing in Canada?

In the codes checked here (British Columbia's Article 9.8.8.5, and Ontario's as summarised by the City of Kitchener), openings in a guard must stop a 100 mm sphere. Every gap must be under 100 mm, just under 4 inches.

Is 4 inches the same as 100 mm?

Not quite: 4 inches is 101.6 mm, so a gap of exactly 4 inches is too wide under a 100 mm rule. Keep every gap under 3 15/16 inches.

Does the calculator make my deck pass inspection?

No. It keeps every gap under 100 mm, but guard heights, loads and climbability have rules too. Check with your municipality's building department.
