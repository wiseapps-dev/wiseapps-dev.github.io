Equal playing time calculator

Enter your sport, how many play at once and how many are in the squad. You get the minutes each player gets and a fair rotation chart you can print, worked out by the same engine as the Benchwise app. It's free and unlimited, and nothing you type leaves your browser.

How the minutes are worked out

The calculator uses the same planning engine as the Benchwise app.

  1. Split the game into stints. Each period is cut into equal stints between the times you can make changes. Two 20-minute halves with a change about every 5 minutes gives 8 stints of 5 minutes.
  2. Count the field time. Spots on the field × game length. With 7 on for 40 minutes, that's 280 player-minutes to share.
  3. Share it out. 280 ÷ 10 players is 28 minutes each on average. Minutes come in whole stints, so 6 players get 6 stints (30 minutes) and 4 get 5 stints (25 minutes). Nobody is more than one stint apart. That's what "fair" means here: equal minutes, to within one sub window.
  4. Keepers stay put. A keeper stays in goal for a whole period, and their time in goal counts as playing time.
  5. Order the stints. The rotation is arranged so nobody sits out too long and changes are spread through the game.

Tips for game day

  • Longer stints mean fewer changes. Changing every 5 minutes is fairer to the minute; every 8 to 10 minutes is calmer on the sideline. Try both and look at the change count.
  • Rotate the extra stint. When minutes can't divide evenly, some kids get one extra stint. Give it to different kids each week. The app tracks this across the season for you.
  • The referee decides. In sports with subs only at stoppages, treat the times as targets and change at the next break in play.
  • Print it. The print button gives a clean chart for a clipboard. Tick off each change as you make it.
