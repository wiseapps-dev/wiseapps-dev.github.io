Privacy

Benchwise does not collect any personal data. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and your squad, games and season minutes stay on your phone. This website uses no cookies and no analytics, and the free calculator runs in your browser.

Last updated 2026-11-23

Benchwise does not collect any personal data.

  • The app has no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your squad, player names, games and season minutes are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • When you share a minutes summary, it goes only where you choose to send it, such as your team chat.
  • Game alerts are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Benchwise is free. The optional Pro unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play: Apple or Google handle the payment, and we never see your card or account. The app keeps a note on your phone that Pro is unlocked, so it works offline, and never sends it anywhere.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The free calculator runs in your browser. Names you type into it stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere or added to the page address.

If you email us for support, we use your email only to reply to you. We don't add it to a mailing list.

Who we are: Benchwise publishes the Benchwise app and this website. Questions: wiseapps.help+benchwise@gmail.com
