Tournament day rotation planner

Plan several short games in one day so every player gets equal minutes across the whole tournament, not just in each game. Pick your sport, squad and number of games: you get a rotation for each game and the day's totals, with keeper turns shared. Free, printable, and nothing you type leaves your browser.

How it balances a whole day

A single game can only be fair to within one stint: when the minutes don't divide evenly, some players get one extra. Over a tournament day those extras add up, and it's easy to give them to the same players every game. The planner plans the games in order with the Benchwise app's engine. Before each game it feeds in the minutes each player has had so far, the way the app balances a season, so the extra stints go to the players who are behind.

With 10 players in 7v7 soccer and four games of 2 × 10 minutes, changing every 5 minutes, each game gives 8 players 15 minutes and 2 players 10. Over the day, 8 players get 55 minutes and 2 get 60, instead of some players getting 60 and others 40. Keeper turns move round the squad the same way, a whole period each.

Tips for tournament day

  • Print it before you leave. Phones go flat at long tournaments. The print button gives one page with the day's totals and a grid for each game.
  • Games are often shorter. Many junior carnivals and gala days play one or two short halves. Set the periods and minutes to match the draw.
  • If someone leaves early, re-run the remaining games with the smaller squad, or let the Benchwise app re-plan on the spot.

Questions

Does every player get exactly the same minutes?

As close as whole stints allow. Across the day, nobody is more than one stint apart, and the planner gives each game's extra stints to the players who have had less so far.

Who goes in goal?

In formats with a keeper, each turn in goal is a whole period, and the turns go to players who haven't kept goal yet that day. Untick the goalkeeper box to plan without one.

Is anything saved?

No. The plan is worked out in your browser and nothing you type is uploaded or stored. Print it, or plan again next time.
