7-a-side hockey rotation for 8 players

With 8 players in 7-a-side hockey, each player plays 35 of the 40 minutes; 1 sits out each stint. The game splits into eight 5-minute stints, and the minutes divide exactly. There are 7 change times, and Player 1 and Player 2 each keep goal for a half.

The maths

7 spots × 40 minutes = 280 player-minutes. 280 ÷ 8 players = 35 minutes each on average. Minutes come in 5-minute stints (56 stints to share). It divides exactly, so everyone gets the same. Nobody sits out more than 5 minutes in a row. Everyone plays in both halves.

About 7-a-side hockey

Hockey uses rolling substitutions, so changes can be made on the clock (not during a penalty corner). Junior small-sided formats vary in length and numbers by association, so treat 2 × 20 minutes as a starting point.
