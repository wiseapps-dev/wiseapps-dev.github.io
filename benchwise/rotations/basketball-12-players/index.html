Basketball substitution pattern for 12 players

With 12 players in basketball, each player plays 15 or 20 of the 40 minutes; 7 sit out each stint. The game splits into eight 5-minute stints, so four players get one extra stint. There are 7 change times. Nobody sits out more than 15 minutes in a row.

The maths

5 spots × 40 minutes = 200 player-minutes. 200 ÷ 12 players = 16.7 minutes each on average. Minutes come in 5-minute stints (40 stints to share). So 4 get 20 minutes and 8 get 15, which is as even as whole stints allow.

About basketball

In basketball, subs go on at a stoppage, so treat each change time as a target and make it at the next dead ball. FIBA-style games use four 10-minute quarters, but many Australian domestic competitions play two halves of about 18 to 20 minutes with a running clock. See youth basketball substitution patterns.
