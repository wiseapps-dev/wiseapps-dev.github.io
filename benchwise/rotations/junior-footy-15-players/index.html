Junior footy rotation for 15 players

With 15 players in junior footy, each player plays 36 or 42 of the 48 minutes; 3 sit out each stint. The game splits into eight 6-minute stints, so six players get one extra stint. There are 6 change times.

The maths

12 spots × 48 minutes = 576 player-minutes. 576 ÷ 15 players = 38.4 minutes each on average. Minutes come in 6-minute stints (96 stints to share). So 6 get 42 minutes and 9 get 36, which is as even as whole stints allow. Nobody sits out more than 12 minutes in a row. At 75% to 88% of the game, every player is within or above the 50 to 75% minimum range in the national junior rules.

About junior footy

This chart uses 12 on the ground and four 12-minute quarters. The AFL's national junior rules use that for under 10. They suggest rotating players at least every quarter, including through the interchange, and a minimum of 50 to 75% game time, with the exact figure set by your league. See junior footy rotations.
