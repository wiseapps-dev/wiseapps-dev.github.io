11-a-side hockey rotation for 15 players

With 15 players in 11-a-side hockey, each player plays 40 or 45 of the 60 minutes; 4 sit out each stint. The game splits into twelve 5-minute stints, so twelve players get one extra stint. There are 11 change times, and Player 10, Player 11, Player 12 and Player 13 each keep goal for a quarter.

The maths

11 spots × 60 minutes = 660 player-minutes. 660 ÷ 15 players = 44 minutes each on average. Minutes come in 5-minute stints (132 stints to share). So 12 get 45 minutes and 3 get 40, which is as even as whole stints allow. Nobody sits out more than 10 minutes in a row.

About 11-a-side hockey

Hockey uses rolling substitutions, so changes can be made on the clock (not during a penalty corner). Full-field hockey is played in four 15-minute quarters at senior level. Junior games are often shorter, so set your times in the calculator.
