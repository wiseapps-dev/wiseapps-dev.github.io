11v11 soccer rotation for 15 players

With 15 players in 11v11 soccer, each player plays 49 or 56 of the 70 minutes; 4 sit out each stint. The game splits into ten 7-minute stints, so five players get one extra stint. There are 9 change times, and Player 12 and Player 13 each keep goal for a half.

The maths

11 spots × 70 minutes = 770 player-minutes. 770 ÷ 15 players = 51.3 minutes each on average. Minutes come in 7-minute stints (110 stints to share). So 5 get 56 minutes and 10 get 49, which is as even as whole stints allow. Nobody sits out more than 14 minutes in a row. Everyone plays in both halves.

About 11v11 soccer

Game length for 11v11 youth football varies by age and competition, so treat 2 × 35 minutes as a starting point. Some competitions limit changes to stoppages with the referee's permission. Check your rules, then set the times in the calculator.
