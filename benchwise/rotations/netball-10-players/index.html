Netball rotation for 10 players

With 10 players in netball, each player plays 20 or 30 of the 40 minutes; 3 sit out each quarter. Changes happen only at the quarter breaks, so time comes in whole quarters, and eight players get one extra quarter. There are 3 change times.

The maths

7 spots × 4 quarters = 28 quarters of court time to share. 28 ÷ 10 players = 2.80 quarters each on average. So 8 get 30 minutes and 2 get 20, which is as even as whole quarters allow. Nobody sits out more than 20 minutes in a row. Nobody plays the same position twice in the game.

About netball

Under the current Rules of Netball, changes are made at the breaks, after a goal if the umpire holds time, or for injury, illness or blood. This chart makes changes at the quarter breaks only, so each stint is a whole quarter. NetSetGO allows unlimited changes at any time. See netball positions for juniors.
