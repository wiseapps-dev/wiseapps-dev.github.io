Season playing time tracker

Type the minutes each player played in each game and the tracker adds up the season: games, minutes, each player's fair share of the field time and who is owed time. It works for any sport. Nothing is uploaded or stored: save the table as a CSV file and open it again next week.

What counts as fair

A player's fair share of a game is the field time shared equally among the players who turned up: players on the field × game length ÷ players there. With 7 on for 40 minutes and 10 players there, that's 28 minutes each. A player who missed a game isn't owed time for it.

The table adds up each player's games and minutes with the same season code as the Benchwise app, then compares the minutes played with the fair share. Players below 90% of their fair share are marked as owed time; above 110% as ahead. Start the players who are owed time next game, or give them the extra stint.

Keeping it from week to week

Nothing is saved on this page or sent anywhere. When you finish, press Save as CSV to download the table, and next week press Open a CSV to carry on. The file opens in any spreadsheet too. The Benchwise app records minutes automatically from the game clock and balances the next rotation for you.

Questions

What if a player was there but didn't play?

Type 0. An empty box means the player wasn't there, so that game doesn't count towards their fair share.

Do keepers' minutes count?

Yes. Time in goal is playing time, so type the whole time the player was on the field.

Is my squad saved?

No. Names and minutes stay in this browser tab and are never uploaded or stored. Save the CSV file to keep them.
