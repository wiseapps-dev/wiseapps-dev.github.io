Substitution timer

A free game clock for coaches that beeps at every planned substitution. Pick your sport, the period length and how often you change, press Start at the whistle, and it counts down to each change and to the end of the period. It runs in your browser; keep the page open with the screen on.

How to use it

  1. Pick your sport, then check the periods, their length and how often you want to change. The change times come from the same engine as the Benchwise app, so they match its rotation charts.
  2. Press Start at the whistle. The clock counts up; underneath, it counts down to the next change. At each change it beeps twice and, on Android phones, vibrates.
  3. Pause when the referee stops the clock, and use −10 s or +10 s if you started late. At the end of each period it beeps three times and stops; press Next period after the break.

The clock keeps time from timestamps, so it stays right even if the screen sleeps, but the beeps only sound while this page is open and the phone is unlocked. “Keep the screen on” asks the browser to stop the screen sleeping while the clock runs; not every browser allows it. Turn the volume up: phones on silent may not beep.

The timer tells you when to change. For who, print a chart from the playing time calculator, or let the Benchwise app show who comes off and who goes on at every alert.

Questions

Can it beep when my phone is locked?

No. A web page can't make sounds while the phone is locked. Keep this page open with the screen on. The Benchwise app also sends a notification at each change.

What about sports with subs only at stoppages?

Treat each beep as a target and change at the next dead ball. The referee decides.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, yes: everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
