Guides for junior sport coaches

Practical guides for volunteer coaches: how to give every kid equal playing time, rotation plans for soccer, basketball, netball and junior footy, and how to talk to parents about minutes. Each one covers the rules that apply in Australia and where they vary by club or competition.

  • How to Give Every Kid Equal Playing TimeEqual playing time in junior sport, step by step: count the minutes, split the game into equal stints, rotate the extra stint, and handle late arrivals.
  • A Soccer 7v7 and 9v9 Substitution PlanEqual-minutes sub plans for 7v7 and 9v9 junior soccer: MiniRoos formats, rolling subs, keeper turns, minutes for every squad size and a 10-player rotation.
  • Netball Positions for Juniors and How to Rotate ThemThe seven netball positions and where each can go, NetSetGO rules, when changes are allowed, and a sample quarter-by-quarter rotation for 9 players.
  • Basketball Substitution Patterns for Youth TeamsFair sub patterns for junior basketball: minutes for squads of 6 to 12, subbing at stoppages, quarters or running-clock halves, and playing time rules.
  • Junior Footy Rotations: Fair Game Time in Aussie RulesHow to rotate a junior Aussie rules team through the interchange and zones, with the national junior rules on team sizes, quarters and minimum game time.
  • Talking to Parents About Playing TimeHow volunteer coaches can set playing time expectations before the season, share the minutes, and handle a worried parent calmly, with messages to copy.
