Fair sub rotations for kids' sport

Benchwise works out who comes on and off so every player gets equal minutes. Pick your sport, format and squad size to open a free, printable rotation chart. On game day the app runs it live: a game clock, an alert at every change, and a new plan when someone arrives late.

This is what fair looks like

Ten players in 7v7 soccer, two 20-minute halves. Six play 30 minutes and four play 25: one stint apart, as even as whole stints allow. Ruby and Sam each keep goal for a half, and that counts as playing time.

On the sideline, the app runs it live

  • A game clock with an alert at every change, showing who comes off and who goes on.
  • Someone late or hurt? It re-plans the rest of the game in a tap, and keeps it fair.
  • Minutes add up across the season, ready to share with parents.
