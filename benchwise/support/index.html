Support

Email wiseapps.help+benchwise@gmail.com and a real person replies, usually within two business days. For a rotation question, include the sport and format settings, how many players were here, and what you expected. Answers to the most common questions about the app are below.

Common questions

Is it a subscription?

No. Benchwise is free to download. One optional in-app purchase, Benchwise Pro (US$6.99 once, or the local equivalent), unlocks live game mode, season minutes and more than one team. There are no ads and no account.

What is free?

One team, the fair rotation planner, the whole plan as a chart, and printing or sharing it. Benchwise Pro adds the live game clock with change alerts, re-plans for late arrivals and injuries, season minutes and more teams.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The planning, the game clock and the alerts all run on your phone, so it works at a ground with no signal.

What if a kid turns up late?

Tick them in when they arrive. The rest of the game is re-planned straight away so they get a fair share of the time that's left. If your team prefers, you can have late arrivals catch up minutes instead.

What about injuries?

Take the player off and mark them out. Someone from the bench goes on, and the remaining stints are shared fairly among whoever is still available. If they come back, tick them in again.

Does it work for netball positions?

Yes. The netball preset uses the seven positions (GS, GA, WA, C, WD, GD, GK) with changes at the quarter breaks. Each player gets a spread of positions over the game and the season, and you can tick which positions each player can play.

How is fairness worked out?

Each period is split into equal stints between the times subs can happen. The stints are shared as evenly as possible among the players who are here, so everyone's minutes are equal or within one stint (one sub window) of each other. Time in goal counts. Across the season, kids who are behind on average get the extra stint.

Is there an Android version?

The iPhone version comes first. Android follows.

The alert didn't go off

Keep Benchwise open with the clock running: the screen stays on and you get a beep and a buzz at every change. Check that the sound and vibrate settings are on in Benchwise. Lock-screen notifications are only a backup, and the phone's Focus or silent settings can hold them back.

My team or season minutes disappeared

Everything is stored on the phone. It is removed if you delete the app. Share the season summary now and then to keep a copy.

The rotation looks wrong

Email us the format settings, how many were here and what you expected. We test the planner carefully and will explain or fix it quickly.

New phone, or reinstalled the app?

Open Settings in Benchwise, or tap any feature marked Pro, and tap Restore purchases. Pro comes back on the same Apple ID or Google account at no charge.

Refunds

Apple handles App Store refunds: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play refunds go through your Google Play order history.
