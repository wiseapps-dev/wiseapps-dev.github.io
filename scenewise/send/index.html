How it works

  1. Paste the script and tap Find the characters. It uses the same script reader as the Scenewise app.
  2. Check the names. If one person appears under two spellings (NELL and NELLIE), merge them. Rename anything that came out wrong.
  3. Add the details. Who it's from, who it's prepared for (shown on their copy), and a note, like an off-book date or a call time.
  4. Create the link and paste it into the cast chat, an email or a text. Make one per actor if you want each copy to show their name and pick their part.

What the actor sees

The link opens a page showing the title, who it's from, your note and the characters. With the Scenewise app, they tap once and the script opens ready to rehearse. Without it, they can rehearse their first 6 lines in the browser: it reads the other parts aloud and hides their lines.

Privacy and passwords

  • Nothing is uploaded. The script is compressed and packed into the link, after the #. Browsers never send that part of a link to any server, so the script never reaches us.
  • Anyone with the link can read the script, so treat it like an attachment. For unreleased or confidential material, add a password.
  • Passwords encrypt the script in your browser (AES-GCM). Send the password a different way from the link. We can't recover a lost password, and neither can anyone else.
  • Only send what you're allowed to share, like sides from casting, your own writing, or a script your school or company has the rights to.

Long scripts

A short scene makes a link of a few thousand characters. A full-length play can run past 60,000, which some apps cut short. For those, download the .scenewise file and attach it to an email. On iPhone, tapping the file opens it in Scenewise.
