What is a cast link?

An agent, drama teacher, director or scene partner prepared this script in Scenewise and sent it to you as a link. The whole script, with its characters and scenes sorted out, is packed inside the link itself.

  • Got the app? Tap Open in the Scenewise app. The script opens ready to rehearse, with your part picked if the sender chose one.
  • No app yet? Rehearse in your browser reads the other parts aloud and hides your lines, for your first 6 lines.
  • Asked for a password? The sender gives it to you separately. Nobody else, including us, can recover it.
