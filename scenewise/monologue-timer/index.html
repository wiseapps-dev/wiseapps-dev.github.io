    How it estimates the time

    It counts the spoken words, leaving out stage directions in brackets and a character name in capitals at the start of a line, and divides by a speaking pace. 140 words a minute is a steady, unhurried pace for a speech and the figure the Scenewise app uses for your lines; nerves usually push actors faster. The stopwatch tells you your real pace: say it aloud at performance speed, with the pauses, and press Stop at the last word.

    Fitting a time limit

    Auditions often set a limit, commonly one or two minutes, and panels may stop you when it runs out. If you're over, cut whole sentences rather than rushing: the pauses are where the acting is. Check the audition brief for the exact limit and whether it includes your introduction. It runs in your browser: nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.
