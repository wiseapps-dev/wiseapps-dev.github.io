Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Scenewise does not collect any personal data. The app has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking, and your scripts, recordings and progress stay on your phone. This website uses no cookies or analytics, and the free tools and cast links run entirely in your browser, so nothing you paste is sent to us.

The app

  • There's no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your scripts, progress and settings are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The voices are your phone's own text-to-speech voices, and the app works with no internet connection.
  • The camera and microphone are used only when you choose a feature that needs them: self-tapes, hands-free listening, or recording the reader. iOS asks your permission first.
  • Hands-free listening only measures loudness, to tell when you've finished a line. There's no speech recognition and nothing is saved.
  • Self-tape takes and recorded lines are kept on your phone until you save or share them.
  • If you share a script or copy text, it goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Scenewise is free. The optional Pro unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play: Apple or Google handle the payment, and we never see your card or account. The app keeps a note on your phone that Pro is unlocked, so it works offline, and never sends it anywhere.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

This website

  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics, and loads nothing from other companies' servers.
  • The free tools run entirely in your browser. The text you paste is never sent to us. Close the page and it's gone.
  • Cast links carry the script inside the part of the link after the #. Browsers never send that part to any server, so opening a cast link, or making one on the send page, doesn't upload the script to us or anyone else.
  • The line-runner uses your browser's built-in speech and prefers voices installed on your device. Some browsers also offer online voices run by the browser maker; those follow that company's privacy policy.

If you email us for support, we use your email only to reply to you. We don't add it to a mailing list.
