Import your script or sides and pick your part: your phone reads every other character aloud and hides your lines in stages, from full text to first letters to a blank page, until you can say them with nothing on screen. From the first read to off-book

  1. Add your script

    Open a PDF, Word, Final Draft, Fountain or text file from Mail or Files, paste the text, or tap a cast link. Scenewise finds the characters, the scenes and the stage directions.

  2. Hear your cues

    Pick your part. Your phone reads everyone else's lines aloud, in a different voice for each character where your phone has enough, and stops for yours.

  3. Go off-book

    Your lines fade from full text to gaps, then first letters, then a blank page. Set an off-book date and Scenewise plans a daily drill, weakest lines first.

Before you buy

Is it a subscription?

No. Scenewise is free to download. One optional in-app purchase, Scenewise Pro (US$6.99 once, or the local equivalent in your App Store), unlocks the rest. There's no subscription, no ads and no account.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Free: the sample scene, one script of your own at a time with every rehearsal drill and the typed check, and every script someone sends you as a link, always. Pro, a one-time unlock: unlimited scripts, self-tape with the reader, recording the reader, the off-book planner, sending links and .scenewise files, and the cue script.

Are the voices real actors?

No. Scenewise uses the text-to-speech voices built into your phone. They're clear and never tire of running the scene, but they don't act. The free Enhanced and Premium voices sound far better than the defaults, and you can record the other parts yourself, or have a friend do it, for a real voice.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Importing, the voices and the drills all run on your phone, so it works backstage, on a train or in flight mode. If you want an Enhanced voice, download it once over Wi-Fi.

Which files can I import?

PDF, Word (.docx), Final Draft (.fdx), Fountain and plain text, or paste text from an email or your notes. Scanned PDFs are pictures of pages with no text in them, so ask for a text PDF or a Word file. Save old .doc, Pages and RTF files as .docx or PDF first.

Does it listen to me say my lines?

Only for loudness, and only if you choose to. Hands-free mode and the self-tape reader use the microphone to tell when you've stopped talking. There's no speech recognition, and nothing is sent anywhere. Otherwise you mark each line yourself or type it.

My phone is on silent. Will I still hear it?

Yes. Scenewise plays its voices the way a music app plays audio, so it speaks with silent mode on. If you hear nothing, turn up the volume with the side buttons and check you're not connected to Bluetooth headphones.

Someone sent me a cast link. What do I do?

Tap it. If you have Scenewise, choose Open in the Scenewise app and the script opens ready to rehearse, with your part picked. If you don't, you can rehearse your first 6 lines in your browser. If it asks for a password, the sender will give it to you separately.

Can I use it for a monologue?

Yes. Paste or import the speech and tap "It's a monologue". Each paragraph becomes one of your lines, and the drills work the same way. The free first-letter tool is handy for monologues too.

Is there an Android version?

The iPhone version comes first. Android follows.
