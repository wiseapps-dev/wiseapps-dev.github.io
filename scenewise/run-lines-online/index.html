How the line-runner works

  1. Paste a scene or tap Try the sample scene. The same parser as the app finds the characters, the scene headings and the stage directions.
  2. Pick your part and how much of your lines to show: full text, some words hidden, most words hidden, first letters, or blank.
  3. Press Start. Your browser reads the other characters' lines. When it's your turn, it stops. Say your line out loud, tap Reveal to check, then Next.

It uses your browser's built-in speech, so the voices depend on your device. If your browser can't speak, the other lines show on screen with a pause instead. Words in brackets are shown but never read aloud or hidden.

What's free and what's in the app

The online version runs your first 6 lines of any script, as often as you like. The Scenewise app runs the whole thing, with a separate voice for every character, PDF, Word and Final Draft import, a typed check, saved scripts and a "learnt" score for every scene. It also works with no signal.
