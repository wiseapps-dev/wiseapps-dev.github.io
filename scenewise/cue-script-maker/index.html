How to use it

  1. Paste your script or a scene, or tap Try the sample scene. The same parser as the Scenewise app finds the characters, the scene headings and the stage directions.
  2. Pick your part. The cue script lists every one of your lines with only the end of the line before it: the cue you listen for.
  3. Choose how much to show. Full lines to learn from, first letters to test yourself, or blank to say each line from memory with only the cue in front of you.
  4. Print it or save it as text for your phone. It runs in your browser: nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.

What a cue script is

Actors in Shakespeare's company were given only their own part: each speech with the last two or three words of the line before it. They learned to listen for those words instead of reading along. It still works: a cue script cuts a long play down to the lines you have to know and the moments you have to come in, and it stops you learning everyone else's lines by accident.

To hear the other parts read aloud and come in on cue, try run lines online. To see who is in which scene, use the script breakdown.
