Wardrobe guides

Short, practical guides to owning fewer clothes and wearing them more: how to build a capsule wardrobe, what cost per wear tells you, how to photograph clothes for a closet app, how to rotate sneakers and how to clear out what you haven’t worn in 90 days.

  • How to build a capsule wardrobeHow to build a capsule wardrobe from clothes you already own: count what you wear, pick base colours, set a number, fill gaps slowly, review each season.
  • Cost per wear explainedCost per wear is the price divided by the times you wear it. How to work it out, why cheap clothes can cost more per wear, and how to use it to shop.
  • How to photograph clothes for a closet appGet clean cut-outs of your clothes: a plain, contrasting background, soft daylight, the whole piece in frame, and a quick touch-up for any leftovers.
  • Sneaker rotation and rest daysWhy rotating sneakers helps them last, how many rest days to give leather, suede and running shoes, and a simple way to see which pairs are ready to wear.
  • How to declutter with a “not worn in 90 days” listHow to declutter your wardrobe with a 90-day list: find what you haven't worn, decide piece by piece, and keep the list honest by logging what you wear.
