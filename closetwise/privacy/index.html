Privacy policy

Closetwise collects no data. Your closet, photos, outfits, wears and trips are stored only on your phone, and the app never sends them anywhere: no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to export or share it.

The app

  • Everything you add is stored in the app’s own storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Background removal runs on the phone, with a model that ships inside the app.
  • The optional Closetwise Pro unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies; the app only asks the store whether Pro is unlocked, and receives no payment details. We never see who bought it.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.
  • The camera and photo library are used only when you add a picture, and only with your permission. “Save Image” from the share sheet asks to add to your photo library.
  • Exports and shares (a backup file, an outfit picture) go only where you send them.
  • Your phone’s own backups may include the closet: on iPhone, iCloud device backups include the app’s documents; on Android, Auto Backup can include up to 25 MB. These are your backups, under your Apple or Google account, not data we collect.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, fonts or scripts from other companies. The packing list generator and calculator run in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. The site remembers in your browser, for the visit, whether you closed the small app banner.
