Free

Free to start. Unlock once.

Most closet apps are subscriptions. Closetwise is free to download, with up to 15 pieces, 3 outfits and a packing list, plus cut-outs, wear logging, cost per wear on every piece, the shoe rack and backups. Closetwise Pro unlocks everything once for US$4.99: unlimited pieces, outfits and packing lists, the outfit calendar and Insights, with the clean-out list. No subscription, no ads, no account, and Pro is yours to keep.

Private by design

Your photos stay on your phone. The background is removed by a small model that runs on the phone itself, only when you add a picture. The app sends nothing anywhere, has no analytics and collects no data. The optional Pro unlock is handled by Apple or Google.

Move to a new phone with one backup file that you export and keep.

Shoes and packing, built in

A shoe rack of every pair, with optional rest days for leather and suede so you know which pairs are ready. Log a wear in one tap.

Packing lists from the trip length, laundry, weather and what you’ll do, filled from your own closet. Tick things off as you pack.
