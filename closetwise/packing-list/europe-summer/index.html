Europe in summer packing list

For two weeks in Europe in summer, with hot city days and evenings out, pack for 7 days and wash once: 8 pairs of underwear, 8 pairs of socks, 7 tops, 3 bottoms, 2 dresses, 2 pairs of shoes, 2 sets of sleepwear, 2 bags and sunglasses or a sun hat, plus sunscreen.

Shoes do the heavy lifting

City days mean long walks on old streets. The rules give 2 pairs: one everyday pair you have already worn in, and one for evenings. Leave new shoes at home.

Cover up when you need to

Some churches and cathedrals ask visitors to cover their shoulders and knees. A light shirt or scarf in your day bag means you never have to skip one. Check the places you plan to visit.

Wash once, around day seven

With laundry ticked, a 14-day trip is packed like a 7-day one. Without laundry it would be 14 tops and 15 pairs of underwear.

Dresses for evenings out

One dress for every four evenings out gives 2. They double as day wear in the heat, which makes them the best value pieces in the bag.
