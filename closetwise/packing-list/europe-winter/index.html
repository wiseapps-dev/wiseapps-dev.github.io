Europe in winter packing list

For ten days in Europe in winter, with cold, rainy city days and evenings out, pack 11 pairs of underwear, 11 pairs of socks, 10 tops, 4 bottoms, 3 dresses, 3 knits, 2 jackets or coats, 2 pairs of shoes, 3 sets of sleepwear, 2 bags and 2 cold-weather accessories, plus an umbrella.

Two coats: one warm, one for rain

Cold adds a warm coat and rain adds a rain jacket: 2 in all. A rain shell that fits over a knit is lighter than a second heavy coat.

Layer instead of bulk

One knit for every four cold days gives 3. Thin layers under a coat keep you warm outside and comfortable in heated museums and trains.

Laundry would save a lot

Tick "I can do laundry" and the list packs for 7 days instead of 10: 7 tops instead of 10, and 8 pairs of underwear instead of 11.

Hat, scarf and gloves

Cold weather adds 2 accessories: choose from a hat, scarf and gloves. They weigh almost nothing and do more for warmth than an extra jumper.
