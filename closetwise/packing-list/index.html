How the quantities are worked out

The generator runs the packing rules from the Closetwise app, unchanged:

  • Days to pack for are the trip days, capped at 7 if you can do laundry.
  • Underwear and socks: one a day, plus a spare. Tops: one a day.
  • Bottoms: one for every 3 days, plus a smart pair for work.
  • Dresses: one for every 4 evenings out, plus one for a formal event.
  • Knitwear: one for every 4 cold days, or one for cooler evenings in mild weather.
  • Outerwear: a warm coat for cold, a rain jacket for rain, a blazer for work, at most 3.
  • Shoes: an everyday pair, plus one for each special activity, at most 4.
  • Swimwear: two from 4 days on, so one can dry. Sleepwear: one for every 4 days.

Weather and plans add a few essentials, such as sunscreen for heat, an umbrella for rain, a beach towel, a water bottle for hiking and a laptop for work.

Rules are a starting point. Untick what you won’t need, and read how to build a capsule wardrobe to make every piece work with the others.
