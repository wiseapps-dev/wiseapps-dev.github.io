One month packing list with laundry

For a month away with laundry, in mild, rainy weather with city and gym days, pack for 7 days and wash weekly: 8 pairs of underwear, 8 pairs of socks, 7 tops, 3 bottoms, 1 knit, 1 jacket or coat, 2 pairs of shoes, 3 activewear sets, 2 sets of sleepwear and 1 bag, plus an umbrella.

A month is four washes

With laundry ticked, the rules pack for up to 7 days. Over 30 days that is about four washes. Without laundry the list would ask for 30 tops and 31 pairs of underwear.

Gym days need their own kit

Gym adds activewear, one set for every three days (3), and a second pair of shoes for training: 2 pairs in all.

One rain jacket

Rain adds a rain jacket, and the mild-weather jacket is dropped when it rains, so 1 layer of outerwear. Add the one knit for cooler evenings.

Rotate what you have

Over a month, the same few pieces get a lot of wear. Wash gently and rotate tops evenly, so nothing comes home worn out while the rest looks new.
