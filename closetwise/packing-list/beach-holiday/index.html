Beach holiday packing list

For a week-long beach holiday in hot weather, with evenings out, pack 8 pairs of underwear, 8 pairs of socks, 7 tops, 3 bottoms, 2 dresses, 3 pairs of shoes, 2 swimsuits, 2 sets of sleepwear, 2 bags and sunglasses or a sun hat, plus sunscreen and a beach towel. Two swimsuits mean one is always dry.

Two swimsuits, so one can dry

From four days on, the rules give 2 swimsuits. A damp swimsuit in the morning is no fun, and it is the piece most likely to be left on a balcony.

Three pairs of shoes, each with a job

An everyday pair for getting around, sandals for the beach and one smarter pair for evenings: 3 in all. If your everyday pair is a sandal you can walk in, leave one out.

Socks are still on the list

The rules count socks as one a day plus a spare, 8 here. On a week of sandals you will need far fewer: untick what you won't wear rather than packing it.

Sun first

Hot weather adds a sun accessory, sunglasses or a hat, and sunscreen to the essentials. Beach days add a beach towel. Everything else is clothing you already own.
