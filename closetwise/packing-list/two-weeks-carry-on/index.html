Two weeks carry-on only packing list

For two weeks with only a carry-on, pack for 7 days and do laundry once: 8 pairs of underwear, 8 pairs of socks, 7 tops, 3 bottoms, 1 knit, 1 jacket or coat, 1 pair of shoes, 2 sets of sleepwear and 1 bag. Without laundry the same trip needs 14 tops, which no cabin bag holds.

Laundry halves the list

With laundry ticked, the rules pack for up to 7 days and you wash, so a 14-day trip is packed like a 7-day one. Without it, the list grows to 15 pairs of underwear and 14 tops.

Plan the wash

Wash around day six or seven, when you still have a spare of everything. A sink, a little travel wash and a towel to roll the water out of each piece are enough for underwear, socks and tops.

Check your airline first

Carry-on size and weight limits vary between airlines and fares, and some count your personal item too. Check yours before you pack, not at the gate.

Wear the heavy things

Travel in your jacket, your knit and your only pair of shoes. Choose shoes you can walk in all day, because the rules give just 1 pair for city days.
