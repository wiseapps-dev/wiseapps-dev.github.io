Business trip packing list

For a three-day business trip in mild weather, with work days and evenings out, pack 4 pairs of underwear, 4 pairs of socks, 3 tops, 2 bottoms, 1 dress, 1 knit, 2 jackets or coats, 2 pairs of shoes, 1 set of sleepwear and 2 bags, plus your laptop and charger.

One smart pair for work

The rules give one pair of bottoms for every three days plus one smart pair for work: 2 here. Wear the smart pair to meetings and the other on the way.

A blazer is the work layer

Work adds a blazer and mild weather adds a light jacket: 2 in all. Wear the blazer on the plane so it arrives without creases.

Work and evenings share one pair of shoes

Formal events, work and evenings out add one smarter pair between them, not one each. So 2 pairs: everyday and smart.

Bring a bag for after hours

Evenings out add a second, smaller bag (2 in all), so you can leave the laptop bag at the hotel.
