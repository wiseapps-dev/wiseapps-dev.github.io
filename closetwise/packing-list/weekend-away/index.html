Weekend away packing list

For a two-day weekend away in mild weather with an evening out, pack 3 pairs of underwear, 3 pairs of socks, 2 tops, 1 pair of trousers or a skirt, 1 dress, 1 knit, 1 jacket or coat, 2 pairs of shoes, 1 set of sleepwear and 2 bags. Travel in the knit and jacket.

Wear the bulkiest pieces on the way

The list has 1 knit and 1 jacket or coat. Travel in both, along with the heavier of your 2 pairs of shoes, and the bag holds only the light things.

One pair of bottoms does both days

At one pair of bottoms for every three days, a weekend gets 1. Pick one that works with flat shoes in the day and with the evening shoes at night: dark jeans or plain trousers do both.

The evening is the only extra

Evenings out add 1 dress, a second pair of shoes and a small evening bag, so 2 bags in all. If your evening is casual, drop them and the list gets shorter still.

Why a spare

Underwear and socks are one a day plus a spare: 3 of each for two days. The spare covers a late train, a spill or a warmer day than forecast.
