How the outfits are counted

Each top can go with each bottom, and a dress or jumpsuit is an outfit on its own: tops × bottoms + dresses. Every outfit can then be worn with any pair of shoes, and with or without any one layer. So 5 tops, 3 bottoms, a dress, 2 layers and 2 pairs of shoes make (5 × 3 + 1) × 2 × 3 = 96 outfits from 13 pieces.

The count assumes every piece works with every other. That is the idea of a capsule: two or three base colours, so nothing clashes. If a few combinations don’t work for you, the real number is a little lower, but the next-piece advice still holds.

Which piece to add next

A new piece multiplies with everything it goes with, so the biggest gain usually comes from the category you have fewest of. With 5 tops and 2 bottoms, a third bottom adds five top-and-bottom pairs; a sixth top adds only two. The calculator works this out for your numbers.
