Closetwise support

Need help with Closetwise? Most answers are below: cut-outs, backups, moving to a new phone, cost per wear and the clean-out list. If yours isn’t, email wiseapps.help+closetwise@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened, and you will get a reply from the developer.

Is there a subscription?

No. Closetwise is free to download. One optional in-app purchase, Closetwise Pro, unlocks everything once for US$4.99. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Free: up to 15 pieces of your own (the sample closet doesn’t count), 3 outfits and 1 packing list, with cut-outs, wear logging, cost per wear on every piece, the shoe rack and backups. Pro adds unlimited pieces, outfits and packing lists, the outfit calendar (plan looks ahead and see what you wore) and Insights, with the clean-out list. Nothing you add is ever hidden or deleted.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in Closetwise and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start adding pieces, or explore the sample closet first.

Why does a cut-out keep part of the background?

Background removal works best on a plain, contrasting background, such as a door, a wall or a bed sheet. On a busy background it can keep a corner or a hanger. Use Touch up to erase, restore or crop. The original photo is kept, so you can always start again.

How do I move my closet to a new phone?

In Settings, export a backup. It is one .closetwise file with your pieces, outfits, trips and pictures. Send it to yourself, then import it on the new phone.

How is cost per wear worked out?

The price divided by the wears you logged in the app, plus your “worn before” estimate. Pieces without a price or with no wears have no cost per wear.

What is the clean-out list?

Everything you haven’t worn in the last 90 days, plus anything never worn that you added 90 or more days ago. It is part of Insights in Closetwise Pro.

My closet disappeared after I deleted the app

Everything is stored on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it. Restore from a backup file if you exported one, or from your phone’s own backup.

Which languages does it support?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese (Portugal) and Swedish.

Still stuck?

Email wiseapps.help+closetwise@gmail.com. Include your phone model and the app version (Settings, at the bottom).
