Printable outfit calendar

Print a month on one page and note or sketch what you wear each day, with an optional tally of your most-worn pieces. Choose the month, whether weeks start on Monday or Sunday, and A4 or US Letter paper, then print. After a month you will see what you really reach for.

Made in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

A calendar that fills itself in

In Closetwise, “Wore this today” logs every piece of an outfit in one tap, and the calendar shows what you actually wore, with your most and least worn pieces.

Free to download, with one optional unlock. No account, no subscription, no ads.

How to use it

  • Each evening, write or sketch the day’s outfit in its box. A few words is enough: “navy jumper, jeans, white sneakers”.
  • In the tally, list pieces as they come up and add a stroke each time you wear one.
  • At the end of the month, look for the pieces that never appear. They are candidates for the 90-day declutter list.

For the outfits your pieces could make, try the capsule wardrobe calculator.
