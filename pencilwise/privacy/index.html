Privacy policy

Pencilwise collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app makes no network connections of its own. What you enter, such as a child’s first name and their stars, stays on the device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Children’s privacy

Pencilwise is made for children and follows the rules for apps in the App Store’s Kids category. It does not collect, use or disclose personal information from children, so no parental consent is needed under COPPA (United States) or GDPR (EU and UK) for data we don’t receive. There are no ads, no third-party SDKs, no social features and no way for a child to contact anyone.

What stays on the device

  • Each child’s first name (optional, typed by a parent, up to 20 letters), crayon colour, letter style and stars.
  • Your settings: language, sound, voice, animations and checking level.
  • If you buy Pro, a note that Pro is owned (product and transaction ID), so it works offline.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. In the parents’ area, “Delete everything” does the same without deleting the app. Your device’s own backups (iCloud or Google) may include the app’s data; that is your backup, not data sent to us.

Voices, worksheets and purchases

Letter names are spoken by your device’s built-in text-to-speech voices; nothing is recorded. Worksheets are made as PDF files on the device and leave it only if you share or print them. The optional Pro unlock is paid through the App Store or Google Play: Apple or Google handle the payment, and the app only asks the store whether Pro is owned.

The grown-ups check

Buying, restoring a purchase, the parents’ area, links out of the app (support email and this website) and deleting data are behind a check that asks an adult to hold a button for 3 seconds and answer a multiplication written in words.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, fonts or scripts from other companies. The name worksheet maker runs in your browser; the name you type is never sent anywhere. The site remembers in your browser, for the visit, whether you closed the small app banner. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the technical data your browser sends (such as your IP address) to deliver the pages; see GitHub’s privacy statement.
