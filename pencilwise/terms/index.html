The app

Pencilwise is free to download, with capital letters and numbers in the letter style for your device’s language. Pencilwise Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks lowercase letters, every letter style, name practice, up to 6 children and printable worksheets. Letter styles

The US, UK and NSW and ACT styles, the German Grundschrift and French school cursive are drawn to follow the published letter-formation rules of those models; the other Australian states, New Zealand and Spanish school cursive are drawn from openly licensed fonts (SIL Open Font License) made in the style of those models; School print is a common print, not any country’s official model. They are not endorsed by any school, education department, publisher or trademark owner, and schools vary: follow your child’s teacher where they differ. Pencilwise is a practice tool, not an assessment or a diagnosis of handwriting difficulties.
