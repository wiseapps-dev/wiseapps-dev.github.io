How to write a capital K

To write a capital K in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot on the top line. Go straight down, and finish on the baseline. It takes 2 strokes. Other styles differ in slope and stroke order; the diagrams below show each one, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go straight down, and finish on the baseline.
  2. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go down and to the left, then down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.

Capital letters reach from the top line to the baseline. Most start at the top; the downstroke usually comes first, then any slides across.

New Zealand print

  2. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go down and to the left, then down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.
