How to write the number 7

To write a number 7 in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot on the top line. Go across to the right, then down and to the left, and finish on the baseline. It takes 1 stroke. Other countries write it slightly differently; the diagrams below show each style, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go across to the right, then down and to the left, and finish on the baseline.

Numbers are as tall as capital letters. Children often reverse 2, 3, 5, 7 and 9 at first: start at the green dot every time and the reversal fades.
