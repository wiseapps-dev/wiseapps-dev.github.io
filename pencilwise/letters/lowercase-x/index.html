How to write a lowercase x

To write a lowercase x in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot on the middle line. Go down and to the right, and finish on the baseline. It takes 2 strokes. UK, Australian, German and French schools form it with small differences, shown below with animated stroke-order diagrams.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the middle line. Go down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.
  2. Start at the green dot on the middle line. Go down and to the left, and finish on the baseline.

Say the movement out loud while writing (for example “around, up, down” for a). Small letters sit between the middle line and the baseline; tall letters reach the top line; tails go down to the tail line.
