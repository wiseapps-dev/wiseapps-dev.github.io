How to write a capital S

To write a capital S in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go round anticlockwise, then round clockwise, and finish between the middle line and the baseline. It takes 1 stroke. Other styles differ in slope and stroke order; the diagrams below show each one, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go round anticlockwise, then round clockwise, and finish between the middle line and the baseline.

Capital letters reach from the top line to the baseline. Most start at the top; the downstroke usually comes first, then any slides across.
