How to write a capital G

To write a capital G in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go down and to the right, then across to the left, and finish on the middle line. It takes 1 stroke. Other styles differ in slope and stroke order.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go down and to the right, then across to the left, and finish on the middle line.

Capital letters reach from the top line to the baseline. Most start at the top; the downstroke usually comes first, then any slides across.
