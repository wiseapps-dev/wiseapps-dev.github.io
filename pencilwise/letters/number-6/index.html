How to write the number 6

To write a number 6 in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot on the top line. Go in a curve anticlockwise, then straight down, then all the way round anticlockwise, and finish between the middle line and the baseline. It takes 1 stroke. Other styles write it slightly differently.

Numbers are as tall as capital letters. Children often reverse 2, 3, 5, 7 and 9 at first: start at the green dot every time and the reversal fades.
