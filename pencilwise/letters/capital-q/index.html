How to write a capital Q

To write a capital Q in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot on the top line. Go across to the right, and finish on the top line. It takes 2 strokes. Other school styles differ in slope and stroke order; the diagrams below show each one, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go across to the right, and finish on the top line.
  2. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.

Capital letters reach from the top line to the baseline. Most start at the top; the downstroke usually comes first, then any slides across.
