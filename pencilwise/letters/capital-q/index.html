How to write a capital Q

To write a capital Q in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot on the top line. Go across to the right, and finish on the top line. It takes 2 strokes. Other styles differ in slope and stroke order; the diagrams below show each one, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go across to the right, and finish on the top line.
  2. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.

Capital letters reach from the top line to the baseline. Most start at the top; the downstroke usually comes first, then any slides across.

In other styles

UK print

Upright print with round letters, a looped k and a curly y

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish on the top line.
  2. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.

Australia: NSW & ACT

Gently sloped print in the style of the NSW Foundation Style, taught in New South Wales and the ACT

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish on the top line.
  2. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.

Australia: VIC, WA & NT

Beginners’ print in the style of Victorian Modern Cursive, taught in Victoria, Western Australia and the Northern Territory

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go down and to the left, then straight down, then up and to the right, then straight up, and finish on the top line.
  2. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go down and to the right, and finish below the baseline.

New Zealand print

Sloped print in the style of New Zealand school handwriting

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish on the top line.
  2. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.

School print

Upright print (a 1 with a flag, a crossed 7) with the letters of your child’s language. A common print, not a national model

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish on the top line.
  2. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.
