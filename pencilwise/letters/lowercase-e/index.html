How to write a lowercase e

To write a lowercase e in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go across to the right, then all the way round anticlockwise, and finish between the middle line and the baseline. It takes 1 stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go across to the right, then all the way round anticlockwise, and finish between the middle line and the baseline.

Say the movement out loud while writing (for example “around, up, down” for a). Small letters sit between the middle line and the baseline; tall letters reach the top line; tails go down to the tail line.
