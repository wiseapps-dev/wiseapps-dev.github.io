How to write the number 3

To write a number 3 in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go all the way round clockwise, and finish between the middle line and the baseline. It takes 1 stroke. Other countries write it slightly differently; the diagrams below show each style, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go all the way round clockwise, and finish between the middle line and the baseline.

Numbers are as tall as capital letters. Children often reverse 2, 3, 5, 7 and 9 at first: start at the green dot every time and the reversal fades.
