How to write the number 8

To write a number 8 in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish between the top line and the middle line. It takes 1 stroke. Other styles write it slightly differently.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish between the top line and the middle line.

Numbers are as tall as capital letters. Children often reverse 2, 3, 5, 7 and 9 at first: start at the green dot every time and the reversal fades.
