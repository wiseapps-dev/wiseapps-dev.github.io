How to write a cursive p

To write a cursive p in US cursive, start at the green dot on the baseline and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish between the middle line and the baseline. It takes 1 stroke. French school cursive and the pre-cursive styles that lead to it are shown below, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the baseline and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish between the middle line and the baseline.

Each cursive letter starts on the baseline with an entry stroke and ends with an exit stroke that leads into the next letter. Dots and crosses come after the whole word.
