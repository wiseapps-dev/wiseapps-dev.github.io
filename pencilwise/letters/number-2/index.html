How to write the number 2

To write a number 2 in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go round clockwise, then down and to the left, then across to the right, and finish on the baseline. It takes 1 stroke. Other styles write it slightly differently.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go round clockwise, then down and to the left, then across to the right, and finish on the baseline.

Numbers are as tall as capital letters. Children often reverse 2, 3, 5, 7 and 9 at first: start at the green dot every time and the reversal fades.
