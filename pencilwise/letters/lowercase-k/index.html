How to write a lowercase k

To write a lowercase k in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot and follow the numbered strokes. It takes 2 strokes. The UK, Australian (NSW and ACT, and VIC, WA and NT) and New Zealand styles and School print form it with small differences, shown below with animated stroke-order diagrams.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go straight down, and finish on the baseline.
  2. Start at the green dot on the middle line. Go down and to the left, then down and to the right, and finish on the baseline.

Say the movement out loud while writing (for example “around, up, down” for a). Small letters sit between the middle line and the baseline; tall letters reach the top line; tails go down to the tail line.
