How to write the number 0

To write a number 0 in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot on the top line and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish on the top line. It takes 1 stroke. Other styles write it slightly differently; the diagrams below show each one, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line and follow the arrows in one stroke, without lifting, to finish on the top line.

Numbers are as tall as capital letters. Children often reverse 2, 3, 5, 7 and 9 at first: start at the green dot every time and the reversal fades.
