How to write a lowercase g

To write a lowercase g in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go all the way round anticlockwise, then straight up, then straight down, then round clockwise, and finish below the baseline. It takes 1 stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot between the middle line and the baseline. Go all the way round anticlockwise, then straight up, then straight down, then round clockwise, and finish below the baseline.

Say the movement out loud while writing (for example “around, up, down” for a). Small letters sit between the middle line and the baseline; tall letters reach the top line; tails go down to the tail line.
