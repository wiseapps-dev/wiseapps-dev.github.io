How to write a lowercase t

To write a lowercase t in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot and follow the numbered strokes. It takes 2 strokes. The UK, Australian (NSW and ACT, and VIC, WA and NT) and New Zealand styles and School print form it with small differences, shown below with animated stroke-order diagrams.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go straight down, and finish on the baseline.
  2. Start at the green dot on the middle line. Go across to the right, and finish on the middle line.

Say the movement out loud while writing (for example “around, up, down” for a). Small letters sit between the middle line and the baseline; tall letters reach the top line; tails go down to the tail line.

In other styles

UK print

Upright print with round letters, a looped k and a curly y

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go straight down, then in a curve anticlockwise, and finish on the baseline.
  2. Start at the green dot on the middle line. Go across to the right, and finish on the middle line.

Australia: NSW & ACT

Gently sloped print in the style of the NSW Foundation Style, taught in New South Wales and the ACT

Same movements as the US print (ball and stick) letter above; the shape is a little different.

Australia: VIC, WA & NT

Beginners’ print in the style of Victorian Modern Cursive, taught in Victoria, Western Australia and the Northern Territory

Same movements as the US print (ball and stick) letter above; the shape is a little different.

New Zealand print

Sloped print in the style of New Zealand school handwriting

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go down and to the left, then in a curve anticlockwise, and finish between the middle line and the baseline.
  2. Start at the green dot on the middle line. Go across to the right, and finish on the middle line.

School print

Upright print (a 1 with a flag, a crossed 7) with the letters of your child’s language. A common print, not a national model

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go straight down, then in a curve anticlockwise, and finish on the baseline.
  2. Start at the green dot on the middle line. Go across to the right, and finish on the middle line.

US slanted print with tails

Sloped print letters with small tails that lead into cursive

  1. Start at the green dot between the top line and the middle line. Go straight down, then in a curve anticlockwise, and finish on the baseline.
  2. Start at the green dot on the middle line. Go across to the right, and finish on the middle line.
