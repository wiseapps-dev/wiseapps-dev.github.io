How to write a capital U

To write a capital U in US print (ball and stick), start at the green dot on the top line. Go straight down, then round anticlockwise, then straight up, and finish on the top line. It takes 1 stroke. Other styles differ in slope and stroke order; the diagrams below show each one, stroke by stroke.

Step by step

  1. Start at the green dot on the top line. Go straight down, then round anticlockwise, then straight up, and finish on the top line.

Capital letters reach from the top line to the baseline. Most start at the top; the downstroke usually comes first, then any slides across.
