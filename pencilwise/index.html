Handwriting practice in your child’s school style

Pencilwise is a handwriting app for children aged about 4 to 8. Your child watches each letter being written, traces it with a finger from a green start dot, then writes it alone. It checks the start, direction and order of every stroke, in the letter style their school teaches.

Watch, trace, then write

  1. Watch, then trace

    An animated pencil writes the letter stroke by stroke. Then your child traces it, starting at the green dot and following the arrows. A stroke that goes the wrong way gets a gentle hint and a replay.

  2. Write it alone, earn stars

    The guide fades and your child writes the letter on the lines. Pencilwise checks the order, direction and shape of each stroke, with a forgiving tolerance for small hands, and gives up to three stars.

  3. Their own name

    Type a first name and trace it letter by letter, then see it written all together in your child’s own strokes. Make printable worksheets of it too.

16 school letter styles

Schools teach different letter shapes: a k with a loop or without, a y that curls or slants, a 1 with a flag, a crossed 7, exit flicks, cursive loops. Pencilwise draws every letter from its own stroke data, following each model’s published formation rules. No style is endorsed by a school authority; pick the one your child’s teacher uses.
