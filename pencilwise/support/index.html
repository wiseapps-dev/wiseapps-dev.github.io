Pencilwise support

Need help with Pencilwise? Most answers are below: choosing a letter style, opening the parents’ area, strokes that aren’t accepted, voices, restoring a purchase and printing worksheets. If yours isn’t here, email wiseapps.help+pencilwise@gmail.com with your device model and the app version.

Which letter style should I choose?

The one closest to what your child’s school teaches. Ask the teacher, or look at a letter-formation sheet from school. In the parents’ area, tap your child and choose a style; each one shows a sample row. For School print, also choose the language whose letters, example words and voice your child gets. Free: the default style for your device’s language. Every other style comes with Pro.

Is my state’s or country’s style included?

Pencilwise has US print, slanted print and cursive; UK print and pre-cursive; Australian print for every state (NSW and ACT; Victoria, WA and the NT, in the style of Victorian Modern Cursive; Queensland; South Australia; Tasmania); New Zealand print; the German Grundschrift; French and Spanish school cursive; and School print, a common print with German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese or Swedish letters that isn’t any country’s official model. Italian, Polish, Portuguese and Dutch school cursive and the German joined scripts aren’t included.

How do I open the parents’ area?

Tap Parents (top right). Press and hold the button for 3 seconds, then type the answer to the multiplication shown in words. The same check protects buying, restoring a purchase, links out of the app and deleting data.

My child’s strokes aren’t accepted.

In the parents’ area, set Checking to Gentle (the default; Standard is stricter). After three misses on one stroke the app loosens the checking on its own. Make sure your child starts at the green dot and follows the arrows: a stroke drawn backwards gets a hint rather than a tick.

The letters aren’t spoken.

Check that “Say letters aloud” is on in the parents’ area and that the device isn’t muted. Pencilwise uses the device’s own voices; if a language has no voice installed, add one in the device’s settings (iOS: Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices).

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Parents’ area, then Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. Pro is a one-time purchase; there is no subscription.

Can my child’s progress move to a new device?

Not yet. Stars and children are kept only on the device, by design, because nothing is sent anywhere. Pro can be restored on any device with the same store account.

How do I print a worksheet?

With Pro: parents’ area, Worksheets, choose a name, a letter or a whole set and the paper size, then Make the PDF. It opens in the share sheet, where you can print or save it. The free name worksheet maker on this website makes name sheets in any style.

Is Pencilwise safe for children?

There are no ads, no account, no chat, no tracking and no data collection. A child who taps a locked letter sees “Ask a grown-up”; nothing can be bought or opened outside the app without the grown-ups check.

Still stuck?

Email wiseapps.help+pencilwise@gmail.com. Include your device model and the app version (parents’ area, at the bottom). Please don’t send your child’s name or photos.
