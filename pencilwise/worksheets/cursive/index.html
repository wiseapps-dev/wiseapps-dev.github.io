The US cursive alphabet, stroke by stroke

Joined lowercase letters with loops, as taught in US schools from about grade 2. Each letter starts on the baseline with an entry stroke and ends with an exit stroke that leads into the next. Pencilwise draws cursive capitals as sloped print, so the capitals sheet is print, not cursive capitals. Each letter starts at the green dot; the numbers give the order of the strokes. How to use the sheets

  1. On the first row of each letter, point to the green dot and trace the solid letter with a finger, following the numbered strokes.
  2. Trace the dotted row with a pencil, starting each letter at its dot.
  3. On the row of dots, write the letter from each dot without a guide. Spare lines are for writing it alone.

The sky, grass and ground lines match the app: tall letters reach the top line, small letters sit between the dashed middle line and the red baseline, and tails go down to the bottom line.

These letters are drawn in the style of the school model; they aren’t endorsed by any school or education department. Schools differ in small details, so if your child’s teacher writes a letter another way, follow the teacher.
