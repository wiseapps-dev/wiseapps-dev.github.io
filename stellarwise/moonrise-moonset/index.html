    Reading the table

    • Moonrise and moonset are when the top edge of the Moon crosses a level horizon. A dash means it doesn’t happen that day: the Moon rises about 50 minutes later each day, so once a month the rise slips past midnight into the next day.
    • Highest is when the Moon crosses the meridian, due south from the Northern Hemisphere or due north from the Southern Hemisphere, with its height in degrees. That is when you see it through the least air.
    • At 9 pm gives the phase and how much of the disc is lit that evening.

    A full Moon rises around sunset and sets around sunrise; a new Moon rises and sets with the Sun, so it is lost in the glare. A first-quarter Moon is highest around sunset, and a last-quarter Moon around sunrise.
