Using the app and the site

  • Stellarwise and this website are for learning about and enjoying the night sky. Positions and times are calculated carefully and tested against published ephemerides, but they can contain errors, and a phone’s compass and clock can be wrong. Don’t rely on them for navigation or anything where a mistake could cause harm.
  • Never look at the Sun without proper eclipse glasses, and never point binoculars, a camera or a telescope at it without a proper solar filter on the front. Looking at the Sun can permanently damage your eyes. The app and the site are not solar filters.
  • Take care outdoors at night: watch your footing, stay off roads and private land, and dress for the cold.
