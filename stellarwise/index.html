    Point your phone to find it

    Choose Jupiter, Sirius or the Moon and an arrow leads you there. The compass is corrected for magnetic declination with the World Magnetic Model, so it points to true north.

    Phone compasses are usually right to within about 5 degrees, and less near metal. Stellarwise shows how sure it is, how to calibrate with a figure 8, and lets you line it up on the Moon or a bright planet.
