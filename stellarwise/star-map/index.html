Printable star map

Make a map of the whole sky for any date, time and place: tonight from your garden, or the night someone was born. Choose the place and time, give it a title, then print it or save it as a picture. It runs in your browser and is free to use.

    How to read it

    The circle is the whole sky. The middle is straight overhead and the edge is the horizon. North is at the top, and east is on the left, which looks backwards until you hold the map over your head with the top towards north: then it matches the sky.

    Bigger dots are brighter stars. The brightest stars are named, constellation figures join their stars, and any planets above the horizon are marked. Stars are drawn down to magnitude 5.5, about what you see from a dark country garden.

    Printing

    Print fills one A4 or US Letter page. Ink on white uses little ink; the night style is for screens and photo prints. The picture is 1200 by 1600 pixels.
