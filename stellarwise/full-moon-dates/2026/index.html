    2026 at a glance

    13 full Moons in 2026; 1 supermoon, the closest on Thu, Dec 24; a blue moon on Sun, May 31; 2 lunar eclipses.

    A full Moon is the moment the Moon is opposite the Sun in the sky. It looks full for about a day either side, so the night before and the night after are just as good for moonlight walks. It rises around sunset and sets around sunrise.
