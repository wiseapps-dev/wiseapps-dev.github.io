About the names

The names (Wolf Moon, Snow Moon, Harvest Moon and so on) come from North American almanac tradition and are given here by calendar month. The full Moon nearest the September equinox is the Harvest Moon, and the one after it the Hunter’s Moon. In the Southern Hemisphere the seasons behind the names are reversed.

Supermoons and blue moons

A supermoon here is a full Moon less than 360,000 km from Earth, one common definition; it looks up to about 14% wider than the smallest full Moon of the year. A blue moon here is the second full Moon in one calendar month, counted in UTC, so it can fall in a different month where you live.
