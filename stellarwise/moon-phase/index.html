      The eight phases

      Half of the Moon is always lit by the Sun. As the Moon goes round the Earth we see more or less of that lit half: new Moon, waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full Moon, waning gibbous, last quarter and waning crescent. Waxing means growing, waning means shrinking.

      The lit side always faces the Sun. From Europe and North America a waxing Moon is lit on the right; from Australia, South Africa and Argentina it is lit on the left. Near the equator a crescent lies on its back. The calendar above draws the Moon the right way round for the place you choose.
