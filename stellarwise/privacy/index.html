Privacy policy

Stellarwise collects no data. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it makes no network calls of its own. Your location is optional, rounded to about a kilometre and never leaves your phone. Your observing log stays on your phone unless you export it.

The app

  • Every position is worked out on your phone from data inside the app. The app sends nothing to us or to anyone else.
  • Location is used only if you tap Use my location. It is rounded to about 1 km, kept in the app’s settings on your phone and used only to work out the sky. You can pick a city from the built-in list instead.
  • Motion and compass readings are used live to move the sky with your phone. They are not stored.
  • Camera (Pro camera view): the live picture is shown on your screen behind the sky. It is never recorded, saved or sent.
  • Notifications (Pro alerts) are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them.
  • Your observing log and settings are stored only on your phone. A CSV export or a shared poster goes only where you send it.
  • The optional Stellarwise Pro unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies; the app only asks the store whether Pro is unlocked, and receives no payment details. We never see who bought it.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.
  • Your phone’s own backups (iCloud or Google) may include the app’s settings and log. These are your backups, not data we collect.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, fonts or scripts from other companies. The tools run in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. The site remembers the place you chose for the tools in your browser’s local storage, and, for the visit, whether you closed the small app banner. Clear your browser’s site data to remove them.
